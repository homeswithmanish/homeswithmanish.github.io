Cash Flow Finder

Active East Bay listings, screened every week for one question: can the rent cover the mortgage?

Most listings will never cash flow, and that is fine. This page exists for the few that might. I screen active listings across my service area, estimate a conservative market rent for each one, and compute the full monthly cost: principal and interest, property tax, insurance, HOA, and Mello-Roos where it applies.

The number that matters is rent divided by PITI. At 1.0 or above, the rent covers the full monthly cost. Between 0.9 and 1.0, a property is close enough that a price cut or a larger down payment could push it over.

A quick note on the numbers. Rent figures are conservative market estimates, not guaranteed rents. PITI is computed at 20% down and the current 30-year fixed rate unless a listing says otherwise. Vacancy, maintenance, and property management are not included. This page is for screening only and is not financial or investment advice. Talk to your CPA and confirm rents with a local property manager before making an offer.

How the screen works

Every listing on this page passed through the same math, so you can compare apples to apples.

1. Conservative rent estimate

I estimate what the home would actually rent for today, and I use the low end of the range on purpose.

2. True monthly cost

3. Ranked by ratio

Deals are ranked by rent divided by PITI, highest first. Green means the rent covers everything.

Common questions

Whether the rent covers the full monthly cost of owning: principal and interest, property tax, insurance, plus HOA and Mello-Roos where they apply. A ratio of 1.0 means the property breaks even on cash flow before vacancy and maintenance.
At current rates, the screen finds the most candidates in Tracy, Manteca, Lathrop, and Mountain House, where prices are lower relative to rents. Higher-priced markets rarely pass a cash-flow screen, though they can still be strong investments on appreciation.
Each listing carries a conservative market rent estimate. Estimates are a starting point, not a promise. I confirm rents with local property managers before any client makes an offer.
