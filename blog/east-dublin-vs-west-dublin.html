East Dublin vs West Dublin: Where Should You Buy?

Manish Anand, REALTOR® · CA DRE #02247006
Updated October 7, 2026 8 min read

Short answer: Buy in West Dublin if you want an established neighborhood of older single-family homes and fewer layers of extra fees. Buy in East Dublin if you want newer construction in a master-planned community and accept HOA dues and, on some parcels, special taxes. Neither side is better for everyone, and the city does not draw an official line between them. Compare specific addresses, then check the tax bill, the HOA package and the school assignment for each one.

This guide explains what the terms usually mean, what the city's own documents support, and what only an address-level check can settle. It is general information, not financial or tax advice.

Quick take: Dublin has one ZIP code (94568), so price data cannot split East from West. Zillow's typical single-family value for the ZIP was about $1,317,044 in August 2026, down about 6.0% from August 2025. Use the table below to compare the things that do differ.

What do "East Dublin" and "West Dublin" actually mean?

They are informal labels. Wikipedia's history of the city notes that the first housing tracts were built in West Dublin in 1960, turning a rural community into a suburb, and the City incorporated in 1982. Dublin's oldest sites, such as the Murray Schoolhouse (1856) and Old St. Raymond's Church (1859), sit near Dublin Boulevard and Donlon Way. In this guide, "West Dublin" means that older core and the neighborhoods built out from it.

"East Dublin" usually means the Eastern Dublin Specific Plan area. The City describes the plan as a framework for growth on approximately 3,300 acres east of the Camp Parks Reserve Forces Training Area, and planning for it dates to 1993. Communities built there include Wallis Ranch, Jordan Ranch and the Fallon Village subareas, and agents also group Dublin Ranch under the East Dublin label. Because the boundary is informal, ask a listing agent which side of the line a home is on and confirm the neighborhood name on the listing. See the Dublin city guide for the full neighborhood list.

How old are the homes in each area?

West Dublin homes date from the first tracts of 1960 onward, so many are decades old. That often means larger established lots, mature trees and a wider range of renovation quality, along with possible updates to roofing, plumbing, electrical and windows. East Dublin homes are far newer, since the plan area was only entitled in the 1990s and first homes in places like Wallis Ranch were sold in 2016. Newer homes usually mean modern layouts and systems, but also smaller lots in many cases and community rules. Whichever you choose, order the inspection and read the disclosures. The home's age, not its area, drives most maintenance surprises.

Do East Dublin homes have Mello-Roos and HOA fees?

Often, but not always. The City of Dublin lists several Community Facilities Districts (CFDs, commonly called Mello-Roos): CFD 2015-1 for Dublin Crossing, a services district for Dublin Crossing, East Ranch (CFD 2023-1) and Dublin Centre. The city says CFD facilities taxes appear as separate line items on the county property tax bill and recommends asking your escrow or title company about any home you buy. For East Ranch, a news report described a special tax starting at 1.58% of each home's sales price and rising 2% a year, equal to roughly $20,000 to $42,000 a year on that project. That figure applies only to East Ranch and is not typical of Dublin.

Note that Dublin Crossing sits on former Camp Parks land near BART, so "newer" does not always mean "far east." I could not confirm which Eastern Dublin communities carry CFDs from the city's pages, which is why the rule is to check each parcel. Read the preliminary title report and the tax bill, and add any special tax and HOA dues to your monthly budget. For how special taxes work, read how Mello-Roos works, then run the numbers in the property tax calculator and the affordability calculator. The true cost of buying section covers what else to include.

How do schools work across East and West Dublin?

Dublin Unified assigns students by home address. The district says each residential area is assigned to a school at each grade level, and it directs families to its School Locator to enter an address. Families can request intra-district transfers, but approvals depend on space, and a student may be diverted when a school is full. Boundaries can change, so confirm the current assignment before you make an offer.

The district runs two comprehensive high schools, Dublin High and Emerald High. Emerald High (3600 Central Parkway) opened to students in 2023-24, partly to relieve crowding at Dublin High. Middle schools include Eleanor Murray Fallon and Wells, and the district also has Cottonwood Creek TK-8. I do not map neighborhoods to schools here, because the district publishes assignments by address, not by area label. For an overview, read best schools in Dublin.

Which side is better for BART and commuting?

Dublin has two BART stations in the I-580 median. Dublin/Pleasanton opened in 1997, with Dublin to its north and Pleasanton to its south. West Dublin/Pleasanton opened in March 2011 near the I-580 and I-680 interchange. Which is closer depends on the exact address, and some East Dublin homes are a long way from either. This article gives no commute times, since they vary by time of day. Drive the route at the hours you would actually travel before you commit. If you are also weighing a nearby city, see Dublin vs San Ramon for families.

Which side is cheaper?

The data does not say. Zillow's Home Value Index (single-family, middle price tier) for ZIP 94568 was about $1,317,044 in August 2026, versus about $1,400,516 in August 2025, a decline of about 6.0%. Every Dublin address shares that ZIP, so no ZIP-level figure separates East from West. Qualitatively, a newer home in a master-planned community and an older home on a larger lot can land in similar price ranges, while the ongoing costs differ: HOA dues and special taxes on one side, maintenance and updates on the other. Compare total monthly cost for the two specific homes you are considering. Past trends do not predict future prices.

How should you choose between East and West Dublin?

  • Pick the home first, then the area. Two homes a mile apart can differ more than the two "sides."
  • Price the full month. Add property tax, any special tax, HOA dues, insurance and a maintenance reserve.
  • Verify the school assignment for the exact address in the district School Locator.
  • Check the distance to BART and your freeway on the days and times you travel.
  • Read the paperwork: preliminary title report, tax bill, HOA documents and seller disclosures.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between East Dublin and West Dublin?

West Dublin is the older part of the city, where the first housing tracts were built starting in 1960. East Dublin is the Eastern Dublin Specific Plan area, a planned expansion east of Camp Parks that has been built out in master-planned communities such as Wallis Ranch. There is no official dividing line, so ask any agent or listing where they draw it.

Is East Dublin or West Dublin better to live in?

Neither is better for everyone. West Dublin tends to suit buyers who want an established neighborhood and are comfortable with an older home. East Dublin tends to suit buyers who want newer construction and community amenities and are comfortable with HOA dues and, on some parcels, special taxes. Compare specific addresses, not areas.

Do East Dublin homes have Mello-Roos?

Some newer Dublin developments do. The City of Dublin lists several Community Facilities Districts, including Dublin Crossing, East Ranch and Dublin Centre, and each adds a separate line item to the county property tax bill. Whether a particular home is in one is parcel by parcel, so read the tax bill and the title report before you write an offer.

Which Dublin schools will my child attend?

Dublin Unified assigns schools by home address through its School Locator, and boundaries can change. Dublin has two comprehensive high schools, Dublin High and Emerald High, so the same neighborhood label can mean different schools. Confirm the assignment for a specific address with the district before you buy.

Which part of Dublin is closer to BART?

It depends on the address. Dublin has two stations on I-580, Dublin/Pleasanton and West Dublin/Pleasanton, and the West Dublin/Pleasanton station is near the I-580 and I-680 interchange. Check the distance from a specific home to the station you would use rather than assuming by area.

What do homes cost in Dublin, and is East or West more expensive?

Zillow's typical single-family home value for ZIP 94568, which covers all of Dublin, was about $1,317,044 in August 2026. Because the whole city shares one ZIP code, that data cannot split East from West. Newer East Dublin homes often come with HOA dues and sometimes special taxes, so compare total monthly cost, not price alone.
