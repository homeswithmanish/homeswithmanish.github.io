Meet Manish Anand

Manish Anand is a licensed California REALTOR® (CA DRE #02247006) serving the East Bay and Tri-Valley from San Ramon. He came to real estate from a career in Bay Area technology, and he works the way that background taught him to: start with the data, show the client the actual numbers, and let the trade-offs sit in the open rather than behind a sales pitch.

In practice that means a buyer sees what a home really costs once property tax, Mello-Roos or CFD assessments, insurance and commute are priced in — not just the list price. A seller sees how list price maps to net proceeds before the sign goes in the yard. An investor sees the rental yield math on the specific street, not a regional average. The free calculators and city guides on this site are the same tools used in client conversations, published openly so you can run the numbers yourself first.

He works with a deliberately small number of clients at a time across eight communities — San Ramon, Pleasanton, Danville, Dublin, Livermore, Fremont, Tracy and Mountain House — which is what makes the hyperlocal detail possible.
