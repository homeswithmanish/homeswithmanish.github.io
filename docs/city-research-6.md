# City Research — 6 New Service-Area Cities

Research for city-guide pages: Manteca, Lathrop, San Jose, Milpitas, Cupertino, Newark.
Compiled 2026-10-02. All school names, neighborhoods, and transit facts verified via web search.
Price bands are conservative 2026 median single-family ranges.

## Manteca, CA

**County:** San Joaquin County

### School districts
- **Manteca Unified School District** — the primary district, serving Manteca plus Lathrop, French Camp, and Weston Ranch (~25,000+ students, K-12).
- **Ripon Unified School District** — serves a small slice of Manteca's eastern edge.

Notable schools (all Manteca Unified unless noted):
- **Sierra High School** (MUSD) — the highest-ranked high school in Manteca per SchoolDigger, with the strongest ELA/math/science proficiency in the district.
- **East Union High School** (MUSD) — comprehensive high school serving north Manteca.
- **Manteca High School** (MUSD) — the district's oldest high school, serving central Manteca.
- **Veritas Elementary School** (MUSD) — among the top-performing elementary schools in the district, with proficiency above district and state averages.

*Honest note: the district as a whole ranks low statewide (near the bottom quartile of California districts per SchoolDigger), though individual schools like Sierra and Veritas outperform that baseline considerably.*

### Price band
**$560K–$610K** — typical median single-family home value range for 2026. (MLS median sale price ran $585K–$605K through summer 2026; median list price ~$613K with prices down roughly 5–7% year-over-year.)

### Commute
- **Highways:** Manteca sits at the CA-99/CA-120/I-205 corridor — CA-99 runs north-south through town, CA-120 connects east-west at the city's edge, and I-205 carries traffic west over the Altamont Pass toward the Tri-Valley and Bay Area. I-5 is a short drive west via Lathrop.
- **ACE rail:** The Lathrop/Manteca ACE station serves the city with peak-hour commuter trains to San Jose (Diridon) via Tracy, Livermore, Pleasanton, Fremont, and Santa Clara; a new downtown Manteca platform at the Manteca Transit Center is part of the Valley Rail expansion program.
- **Nearest BART:** Dublin/Pleasanton station, roughly 40 miles and a 40–45 minute drive over the Altamont (I-205/I-580).
- **Drive times:** San Jose ~1 hour 15 minutes in typical traffic (~60 miles); San Francisco roughly 1.5 hours off-peak and up to 2+ hours in rush hour (~80 miles); the Altamont Pass corridor is a known peak-hour bottleneck.

### Neighborhoods
- **Downtown Manteca** — historic cottages and midcentury ranches around Main Street and Library Park, with the most walkable dining and city events.
- **Union Ranch** — newer master-planned area with spacious modern homes and maintained landscaping, popular with families for its schools and highway access.
- **Del Webb at Woodbridge** — 55+ active-adult community (1,406 homes) with resort-style amenities: clubhouse, pools, fitness center, and tennis/pickleball.

### Tagline
"Altamont-adjacent value where the Central Valley begins"

### Investor lens
Manteca is a cash-flow-forward market in 2026, not an appreciation play: entry prices in the high $500s sit roughly 40% of Bay Area levels, and rents of $1,800–$2,400 have held flat even as purchase prices fell ~5–7% year-over-year, supporting stable rental demand from commuters and logistics workers. The current buyer's market (rising inventory, frequent price cuts, 40–50 days on market) favors patient investors who can negotiate — but expect slow equity growth until Bay Area demand spills over more aggressively.

### FAQs
**Q: How much does a home cost in Manteca?**
A: The typical median single-family home runs **$560K–$610K** as of 2026 — well under half of Bay Area medians — with townhomes and condos available lower and larger new-build homes above $650K.

**Q: Are Manteca schools good for first-time buyers with kids?**
A: Manteca Unified is the main district and quality varies school by school: schools like Sierra High and Veritas Elementary perform well above the district average, while the district overall ranks below the state average — so check attendance boundaries against school ratings for the specific neighborhood you're considering.

**Q: Can I commute to the Bay Area from Manteca?**
A: Yes, but plan around it: ACE commuter rail runs peak-hour trains from the Lathrop/Manteca station to San Jose, and the Dublin/Pleasanton BART is a 40–45 minute drive over the Altamont; expect 1h15 to San Jose and up to 2h to San Francisco in rush-hour traffic.

**Q: Is Manteca a good place to invest in rental property?**
A: Entry prices in the high $500s combined with stable rents ($1,800–$2,400) make it one of the more cash-flow-friendly options within Bay Area commuting distance, but it is a buyer's market with soft near-term appreciation — best suited to buy-and-hold investors, not flippers.

## Lathrop, CA

**County:** San Joaquin County

### School districts
- **Manteca Unified School District** — serves most of the city (east Lathrop, Mossdale area, Central Lathrop).
- **Banta Unified School District** — serves the River Islands area and west Lathrop.

Notable schools:
- **Lathrop High School** (Manteca Unified) — the city's first and only comprehensive high school, opened in Central Lathrop.
- **River Islands Technology Academy II** (single-school charter district, K-8) — top 25% of California schools, 65.5% ELA / 55.4% math proficiency vs. 48.8%/37.3% statewide.
- **Mossdale Elementary School** (Manteca Unified, Lathrop) — serves the Mossdale Village area.
- **River Islands High School** (River Islands Academies / Banta Unified, Lathrop) — the community's own 9-12 campus.

### Price band
**$650K–$750K** — conservative typical median single-family home value, 2026. (Median single-family sale price $739K over the last 12 months; median list price ~$750K as of spring 2026; ~91 days average on market in a balanced market.)

### Commute
- **Highway access:** I-5 runs north-south through the city; I-205 branches west at the I-5/I-205 interchange toward the Altamont Pass and the Tri-Valley; CA-120 (Yosemite Ave) runs east-west through Lathrop to Manteca and Oakdale.
- **ACE rail:** Lathrop/Manteca station at 17800 Shideler Pkwy; Altamont Corridor Express runs east to Stockton and west over the Altamont to Livermore, Pleasanton, Fremont, and San Jose (about 2 hours Lathrop to San Jose).
- **Nearest BART:** Dublin/Pleasanton (Blue Line), roughly 35-45 minutes via I-205/I-580.
- **Typical drive times:** Stockton ~15-20 min, Livermore ~45 min, Pleasanton/Dublin ~40-50 min, San Jose ~60-70 min. A new North Lathrop ACE/Valley Rail station is planned (2027), plus a proposed Valley Link station at River Islands, which would eventually add a one-seat ride to Dublin/Pleasanton BART.

### Neighborhoods
- **River Islands** — the big one: a ~4,800-acre master-planned community on the San Joaquin River with 11 lakes, the Boathouse Restaurant, and its own charter academy system; building since ~2006 toward roughly 11,000 homes.
- **Mossdale Village (Mossdale Landing)** — 1,161-acre planned community west of I-5 along the San Joaquin River, built around a pedestrian village center with about 1,700 homes, parks, and trail corridors.
- **Central Lathrop** — 1,521-acre specific-plan area north of Mossdale Village; the city's newest growth zone, home to Lathrop High School and the Generations Center, with newer subdivisions going in.

### Tagline
"Master-planned value on the Bay Area's eastern doorstep"

### Investor lens
Lathrop sits at the lowest entry prices of any Bay Area-adjacent city, which keeps rents competitive against the purchase price and produces steadier cash flow than most of the greater Bay Area. Tenant demand is deep and diversified: Altamont commuters plus a large logistics and distribution workforce, since Lathrop and nearby Tracy are a major warehouse hub. Watch the math on new construction: Community Facilities District (Mello-Roos) taxes on newer homes add to carrying costs, so net yield is better on resales; appreciation is steady rather than explosive, tied to Bay Area spillover and the River Islands buildout.

### FAQs
**Q: How much does a home cost in Lathrop?**
A: The typical single-family home sells between about $650K and $750K in 2026, with the median sale price around $700K-$740K. New construction in River Islands runs higher, often into the $800Ks and beyond, while older homes east of I-5 tend to price lower.

**Q: Is Lathrop good for first-time buyers?**
A: Yes, it's one of the most reachable entry points into the Bay Area orbit: median prices run $200K-$400K below comparable Tri-Valley cities, most of the housing stock is newer, and family-oriented schools like the River Islands academies draw first-time-buyer households. Check whether the home sits in a Mello-Roos district, since the special tax affects the monthly payment.

**Q: What is the commute from Lathrop to the Bay Area?**
A: Most commuters drive: about 40-50 minutes to Pleasanton/Dublin, roughly an hour to San Jose off-peak, longer in rush hour over the Altamont. The ACE train at Lathrop/Manteca station offers a car-free option to Fremont, Santa Clara, and San Jose.

**Q: Is Lathrop a good place to invest in rental property?**
A: Entry prices are the lowest of any Bay-adjacent city and rental demand is strong from commuters and logistics workers, so cash flow pencils better here than in most of the greater Bay Area. Favor resales over new builds to avoid the extra special-tax burden, and target 3-4 bedroom family homes near the charter schools and commuter corridors.

## San Jose, CA — South/East San Jose Focus (Evergreen, Blossom Valley, Almaden Valley)

**County:** Santa Clara County

### School districts
- **Evergreen**: K-8 by **Evergreen School District**; high school by **East Side Union High School District**. Notable: **Evergreen Valley High School** (East Side Union HSD, 3300 Quimby Rd) — highly rated, ~97% graduation rate.
- **Blossom Valley**: K-8 by **Oak Grove School District**; high school by **East Side Union High School District**. Notable: **Oak Grove High School** (East Side Union HSD, 285 Blossom Hill Rd); also nearby **Santa Teresa High School** (East Side Union HSD).
- **Almaden Valley**: mostly **San Jose Unified School District** (some northwest pockets in Campbell Union; some southwest in Los Gatos Union). Notable: **Leland High School** (San Jose Unified, 6677 Camden Ave, Almaden Valley) — top-rated STEM school, Blue Ribbon award winner; feeder elementaries include Graystone and Williams (both highly rated).
- Note: none of the three focus areas is in a single K-12 unified district except Almaden's SJUSD zones; Evergreen and Blossom Valley both split elementary and high school across two districts.

### Price band
**$1.5M–$2.5M** — conservative 2026 median single-family range across the three focus areas: Blossom Valley ~$1.5M, Evergreen ~$1.5–$1.6M, Almaden Valley ~$2.3–$2.5M (competitive, multiple offers common on well-priced homes in Leland school zones).

### Commute
- **BART**: Berryessa/North San Jose station (opened June 2020) is the current terminus, a short drive from Evergreen/East San Jose with direct service to the East Bay. Phase II (to Downtown San Jose, Diridon, and Santa Clara) is in early construction with a current service target around 2036–2037.
- **Caltrain**: Blossom Hill and Capitol stations serve South San Jose, with the full-service hub at Diridon Station downtown.
- **VTA Light Rail Blue Line** runs through South San Jose with stations at Capitol, Blossom Hill, Snell, Cottle, and the Santa Teresa terminus; Tamien station connects light rail to Caltrain.
- **Highway access**: US-101, I-680, I-280, CA-87 (Guadalupe Parkway), and CA-85 all serve these areas. Typical drives: South San Jose to downtown ~15–20 min, to Palo Alto/Santa Clara job centers ~25–35 min, to San Francisco ~50–70 min outside peak.

### Neighborhoods
- **Evergreen** — Master-planned hillside community in the southeast foothills with newer-built homes, top-rated schools (Evergreen Valley High), and Evergreen Valley College.
- **Blossom Valley** — Established mid-century ranch-home neighborhood in South San Jose with Oak Grove schools and strong VTA Blue Line access.
- **Almaden Valley** — Affluent southwest San Jose valley with Leland High schools, larger lots, and the Almaden Quicksilver trails and golf country club.

### Tagline
"South Bay schools, bigger lots, and one last price break before the Peninsula"

### Investor lens
Cash flow is thin at these price-to-rent ratios — these are appreciation-and-stability plays, not cash-flow markets. Tenant demand is deep and durable, driven by Silicon Valley tech employment (Apple, Google, Adobe, and Santa Clara chip campuses all within commuting range), with Almaden commanding premium rents and Evergreen/Blossom Valley offering more accessible entry points for single-family rentals.

### FAQs
**Q: What does it cost to buy a home in South/East San Jose?**
A: As of 2026, typical median single-family values run about $1.5M in Blossom Valley, $1.5–$1.6M in Evergreen, and $2.3–$2.5M in Almaden Valley. Dated or smaller homes can come in under those numbers; renovated homes in top Leland or Evergreen Valley school zones run well above.

**Q: Are the schools actually good here?**
A: Yes — schools are the main draw. Evergreen Valley High and Leland High both rank among the Bay Area's top public high schools, and feeders like Graystone and Williams Elementary are highly rated. Verify your specific address's assignment, since districts split across Evergreen, Oak Grove, East Side Union, and San Jose Unified boundaries.

**Q: How is the commute to tech jobs?**
A: Strong by Bay Area standards. US-101, I-280, CA-87, and CA-85 put most Santa Clara County campuses 25–35 minutes away; VTA's Blue Line serves South San Jose directly; Caltrain and future BART connect to Diridon; and SF is roughly an hour by car outside peak.

**Q: Is South San Jose a good investment?**
A: Historically, appreciation has been the story — San Jose homes have roughly doubled each decade over the last 40 years. Expect positive long-term appreciation with steady tech-driven rental demand, not strong monthly cash flow. Almaden offers the most premium tenant pool; Evergreen and Blossom Valley give investors a lower entry point with solid school-driven demand.

## Milpitas, CA

**County:** Santa Clara County

### School districts
- **Milpitas Unified School District** — serves almost the entire city (a small southeastern corner near Ed Levin falls under **East Side Union High School District** boundaries). Milpitas USD is ranked in the top 10% of California districts, ~94% graduation rate, 10,000+ students.

Notable schools (all Milpitas USD):
- **Milpitas High School** (1285 Escuela Pkwy) — the district's comprehensive 9–12 campus
- **Thomas Russell Middle School** (1500 Escuela Pkwy)
- **John Sinnott Elementary School** (2025 Yellowstone Ave)
- **Marshall Pomeroy Elementary School** (1505 Escuela Pkwy)

### Price band
**$1.2M–$1.5M** — conservative typical median single-family home value range for 2026. (County-record median all-home-type sales ~$1.24M as of Aug 2026; single-family detached trades at a premium; prices softened roughly 15–20% year-over-year from the 2025 peak.)

### Commute
- **Milpitas BART station** (Milpitas Transit Center, 1755 S Milpitas Blvd) opened June 13, 2020 as part of the 10-mile Berryessa Extension, linking Warm Springs/Fremont through to Berryessa/North San Jose on the Green and Orange lines.
- **VTA Light Rail Orange Line** runs through Milpitas (co-located at the Transit Center) connecting Alum Rock to Mountain View via Sunnyvale; bus plaza with VTA and AC Transit connections.
- **Freeway access**: I-880 and I-680 both run through Milpitas (I-680 junction just east of the city), and CA-237 borders the city to the west.
- **Drive times (approx., off-peak)**: ~15–20 min to downtown San Jose; ~15 min to Fremont; ~25–35 min to Palo Alto; ~20 min to Santa Clara chip campus corridor.

### Neighborhoods
- **Midtown** — central Milpitas along Main Street/N Milpitas Blvd; older homes mixed with newer mixed-use infill, anchored by the city's Gateway-Main Street Specific Plan.
- **Calaveras Ridge** — hillside area around Calaveras Road on the east side; larger lots and rural-leaning properties near Ed Levin County Park.
- **Great Mall district / Transit Area** — transit-oriented development around the Milpitas BART station and Great Mall; condos and townhomes popular with commuters.

### Tagline
"Silicon Valley's front door — commuter-friendly prices at the Bay Area's crossroads"

### Investor lens
Cash flow is tight on long-term holds at a ~$1.2–1.5M entry, so most investors are playing appreciation and 1031-exchange leverage rather than immediate yield. Tenant demand is durable: Milpitas sits between the Great Mall corridor employers and the north San Jose tech campuses, with BART/VTA access drawing a deep pool of tech renters and commuters, plus strong resale liquidity from first-time-buyer families priced out of San Jose and Cupertino.

### FAQs
**Q: How much does a home cost in Milpitas?**
A: Typical single-family homes trade in the **$1.2M–$1.5M** range in 2026, with condos and townhomes starting closer to the high $800s–$1M. Milpitas runs notably cheaper than Cupertino or Palo Alto, which is its main draw for Bay Area buyers.

**Q: Are the schools good for a first-time buyer family?**
A: Yes — Milpitas Unified is a top-10%-of-California district with a 94% graduation rate, and it's the main district serving the city, so most first-time buyers get Milpitas USD schools without worrying about attendance-boundary surprises.

**Q: What's the commute like from Milpitas?**
A: Strong: the Milpitas BART station (opened 2020) connects to the Bay Area rail network, VTA light rail runs west to Sunnyvale/Mountain View, and I-880/I-680/CA-237 give fast highway access to San Jose (~15–20 min) and Fremont (~15 min).

**Q: Is Milpitas a good place to invest in real estate?**
A: It's an appreciation play more than a cash-flow play at current prices. Expect thin initial cap rates, but tenant demand is steady thanks to nearby tech employment and transit, and the entry point is lower than most Silicon Valley cities, which supports liquidity and long-term upside.

## Cupertino, CA

**County:** Santa Clara County

### School districts
Cupertino is served by two districts:
- **Cupertino Union School District** (K-8)
- **Fremont Union High School District** (9-12), one of California's highest-performing high school districts

Notable schools (all Fremont Union HSD):
- **Monta Vista High School** — consistently ranked among the top public high schools in California; located in the Monta Vista area
- **Cupertino High School** — recently rebuilt campus, strong academics and athletics
- **Homestead High School** — comprehensive high school on the city's east side

### Price band
**$2.9M–$3.4M** (typical median single-family home value range, 2026). Strong seller's market with ~104% sale-to-list ratios.

### Commute
- **Apple is headquartered in Cupertino**: Apple Park and the original Infinite Loop campus are both in the city.
- **Freeway access is excellent**: I-280 runs along the city's northern edge (direct to Palo Alto and San Jose); CA-85 cuts through the western side, connecting to Mountain View and San Jose.
- **No Caltrain station inside Cupertino**; nearby stations in Sunnyvale, Lawrence, and Mountain View are reachable via VTA bus lines and the Via-Cupertino on-demand shuttle.
- **Drive times (off-peak)**: Palo Alto ~15-20 min (12 miles via I-280); Mountain View ~10-15 min; downtown San Jose ~15-20 min.

### Neighborhoods
- **Monta Vista** — Cupertino's most prestigious area, set in the western foothills with valley views, large lots, and the highly coveted Monta Vista High attendance zone; home to McClellan Ranch Preserve and Blackberry Farm.
- **Rancho Rinconada** — established east-side neighborhood of mature trees and 1950s-60s ranch homes (many remodeled), a relatively approachable entry point with quick access to Apple Park, CA-85, and I-280.
- **Garden Gate** — central, walkable neighborhood prized for well-kept single-family homes, family convenience, and proximity to top-rated schools and Cupertino's city center.

### Tagline
"Silicon Valley's school-first hometown, anchored by Apple"

### Investor lens
Cupertino is an appreciation play, not a cash-flow one: at ~$3M entry prices, rents won't cover a mortgage, and sale-to-list ratios above 100% show relentless buyer demand. Tenant demand is deep and resilient, driven by Apple employees and families paying premiums to access the Fremont Union HSD schools, making long-term buy-and-hold the winning strategy.

### FAQs
**Q: What does a typical house cost in Cupertino?**
A: Expect around $2.9M-$3.4M for a typical single-family home in 2026. Even condos and townhomes run well over $1M, and homes routinely sell above asking (sale-to-list ~104%), so budget accordingly.

**Q: Are Cupertino schools really that good?**
A: Yes. Cupertino Union (K-8) and Fremont Union HSD (9-12) are among the top districts in California; Monta Vista High and Cupertino High regularly rank among the state's best public schools. The schools are the primary driver of home values here, but always verify attendance boundaries for a specific address.

**Q: What's the commute like from Cupertino?**
A: By car, it's central: ~15-20 minutes to Palo Alto, ~10-15 to Mountain View, and ~15-20 to downtown San Jose (off-peak; rush hour on I-280/CA-85 adds time). Public transit relies on VTA buses and the Via-Cupertino shuttle to nearby Caltrain stations in Sunnyvale and Mountain View.

**Q: Is Cupertino a good investment?**
A: For appreciation, yes; for cash flow, no. Rents can't support $3M purchase prices, but decades of steady demand from Apple workers and school-driven buyers have made Cupertino one of Silicon Valley's most durable long-term bets. Think 10-year horizon, not cap rate.

## Newark, CA

**County:** Alameda County

### School districts
- **Newark Unified School District (NUSD)** — the public district serving Newark, formed 1964.

Notable schools:
- **Newark Memorial High School** (grades 9-12) — Newark's main comprehensive high school
- **Newark Middle School** (grades 6-8) — formed from the merged Newark Junior High
- **Coyote Hills Elementary School** (K-5) — renamed from James A. Graham Elementary in 2021
- **Lincoln Elementary School** (K-5) — long-running elementary on Bettencourt St

### Price band
**$1.30M–$1.45M** — conservative typical median single-family home value range for 2026. (Homes.com median single-family sale price ~$1.39-$1.40M; Houzeo overall median ~$1.34M.) Median rent ~$3,499.

### Commute
- **Dumbarton Bridge (CA-84)** lands in Newark on the East Bay side (junction with Paseo Padre Parkway/Thornton Ave), giving a direct ~20-30 minute drive to Palo Alto and Stanford across the bay (off-peak; toll bridge).
- **No BART station inside Newark**; nearest are **Fremont BART** (~10 min south) and **Union City BART** (~10-15 min north), both served by AC Transit connectors.
- **Dumbarton Express (DB/DB1) buses** run from Fremont and Union City BART across the bridge to Palo Alto and Stanford Research Park, a commuter-friendly alternative to driving.
- **I-880** runs along Newark's eastern edge, feeding San Jose (~20-25 min south) and Oakland; central Newark to downtown Fremont is roughly 10 minutes.

### Neighborhoods
- **Old Town Newark** — Newark's historic core around Thornton Ave and Newark Blvd, with older homes, small shops, and civic buildings.
- **Birch Grove** — established 1960s-80s suburban neighborhood with tree-lined streets, Birch Grove Park, and Birch Grove Primary School.
- **Shelter Creek** — gated condo/townhome community near the NewPark area, popular with commuters for lower-maintenance living near I-880 and CA-84.

### Tagline
"Bridge-side value with Peninsula paychecks"

### Investor lens
Newark prices run $200K-$400K below neighboring Fremont and far below Palo Alto, while pulling the same tech-commuter tenant pool via the Dumbarton Bridge, supporting median rents near $3,500. Cash flow is tight at current rates given the ~$1.35M entry point, so this is an appreciation-and-rent-growth play backed by chronically low inventory (about 2-3 months of supply), not a cash-flow market.

### FAQs
**Q: How much does it cost to buy a home in Newark?**
A: Typical single-family homes land in the $1.30M–$1.45M band, with the overall median around $1.3M. Townhomes and condos in communities like The Keys or Shelter Creek run lower, often under $1M.

**Q: Are Newark schools good for first-time buyers with kids?**
A: Newark Unified is a solid mid-tier district anchored by Newark Memorial High; the district has consolidated several campuses in recent years, so check current boundaries when house-hunting. Families seeking top-ranked schools often compare across the border into Fremont.

**Q: What's the commute like from Newark?**
A: Excellent if you work on the Peninsula: the Dumbarton Bridge (CA-84) is right in town and Palo Alto is roughly 20-30 minutes off-peak. There is no BART station in Newark itself; you'll drive 10-15 minutes to Fremont or Union City BART, or use the Dumbarton Express bus to Palo Alto.

**Q: Is Newark a good place to invest in rental property?**
A: Tenant demand is strong from Peninsula and Silicon Valley commuters, with median rents around $3,500. Cash flow is thin at current purchase prices and mortgage rates, so investors typically buy for long-term appreciation, steady rent growth, and low vacancy rather than day-one cash flow.
