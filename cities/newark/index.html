🌉 CITY GUIDE

Living in Newark, California

Bridge-side value with Peninsula paychecks

Median SFH: $1.30M–$1.45M

Range reflects recent Zillow ZHVI data · live figure loads when available · see the full market table

Newark is the East Bay's bridge town: the Dumbarton Bridge (CA-84) lands here, putting Palo Alto and Stanford a 20–30 minute drive away — while single-family homes run $1.30M–$1.45M, hundreds of thousands below neighboring Fremont. For Peninsula workers who want a house instead of a condo, Newark is the value play.

With no BART station of its own, Newark trades rail access for bridge access — and chronically low inventory (2–3 months of supply) keeps its market competitive.

Schools in Newark

Newark Unified School District Newark Unified is a solid mid-tier district anchored by Newark Memorial High. The district has consolidated several campuses in recent years, so check current boundaries when house-hunting — families seeking top-ranked schools often compare across the border into Fremont.

GETTING AROUND

Commute & Connectivity

Newark's commute is built around the Dumbarton Bridge — ideal for Peninsula workers, workable for everyone else via I-880.

  • Dumbarton Bridge (CA-84) in-town: Palo Alto ~20–30 min off-peak (toll bridge)
  • No BART in Newark — Fremont BART ~10 min, Union City BART ~10–15 min
  • Dumbarton Express buses from Fremont/Union City BART to Palo Alto and Stanford Research Park
  • I-880 along the eastern edge: San Jose ~20–25 min; central Newark to Fremont ~10 min
NEIGHBORHOODS

Where to Look in Newark

Old Town Newark

The historic core around Thornton Ave and Newark Blvd — older homes, small shops, and civic buildings.

Birch Grove

Established 1960s–80s suburb with tree-lined streets, Birch Grove Park, and Birch Grove Primary.

Shelter Creek

Gated condo/townhome community near NewPark — popular with commuters for low-maintenance living near I-880 and CA-84.

INVESTOR LENS

Newark for Investors

Newark prices run $200K–$400K below neighboring Fremont while pulling the same tech-commuter tenant pool via the Dumbarton Bridge, supporting median rents near $3,500. Cash flow is tight at current rates given the ~$1.35M entry point, so this is an appreciation-and-rent-growth play backed by chronically low inventory — not a cash-flow market.
