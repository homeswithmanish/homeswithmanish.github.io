🍎 CITY GUIDE

Living in Cupertino, California

Silicon Valley's school-first hometown, anchored by Apple

Median SFH: $2.9M–$3.4M

Range reflects recent Zillow ZHVI data · live figure loads when available · see the full market table

Cupertino is Silicon Valley's school-first hometown: home to Apple Park, served by two of California's highest-performing districts, and commanding $2.9M–$3.4M for a typical single-family home. Buyers here are paying for certainty — certainty of schools, of demand, and of long-term value.

With sale-to-list ratios above 100%, Cupertino is a competitive, fast-moving market where preparation and pricing strategy decide outcomes.

Elite schools

Cupertino Union (K-8) and Fremont Union HSD (9-12) rank among California's very best.

Apple effect

Apple Park and Infinite Loop anchor deep, resilient housing demand.

Central location

I-280 and CA-85 put Palo Alto, Mountain View, and San Jose all within ~20 min.

Durable value

Decades of school-driven demand make Cupertino one of Silicon Valley's steadiest bets.

EDUCATION

Schools in Cupertino

Cupertino Union (K-8) & Fremont Union HSD (9-12) Cupertino is served by Cupertino Union School District for K-8 and Fremont Union High School District for 9-12 — both among California's top performers. Monta Vista High and Cupertino High regularly rank among the state's best public schools. Always verify attendance boundaries for a specific address.

  • Monta Vista High School
  • Cupertino High School
  • Homestead High School
GETTING AROUND

Commute & Connectivity

Cupertino is car-central: I-280 and CA-85 ring the city, with Apple campuses in-town and no Caltrain station of its own.

  • Apple Park and Infinite Loop headquarters inside the city
  • I-280 along the northern edge; CA-85 through the west side
  • No Caltrain station in Cupertino — VTA buses and Via-Cupertino shuttle reach Sunnyvale/Mountain View stations
  • Palo Alto ~15–20 min; Mountain View ~10–15 min; downtown San Jose ~15–20 min (off-peak)
NEIGHBORHOODS

Where to Look in Cupertino

Monta Vista

The prestigious western foothills — valley views, large lots, the coveted Monta Vista High zone, McClellan Ranch Preserve.

Rancho Rinconada

Established east-side neighborhood of 1950s–60s ranches (many remodeled) — a relatively approachable entry near Apple Park.

Garden Gate

Central, walkable neighborhood of well-kept single-family homes near top schools and the city center.

INVESTOR LENS

Cupertino for Investors

Cupertino is an appreciation play, not a cash-flow one: at ~$3M entry prices, rents won't cover a mortgage, and sale-to-list ratios above 100% show relentless buyer demand. Tenant demand is deep and resilient, driven by Apple employees and families paying premiums for Fremont Union HSD schools — making long-term buy-and-hold the winning strategy on a 10-year horizon, not cap rate.
