🌊 CITY GUIDE

Living in Lathrop, California

Master-planned value on the Bay Area's eastern doorstep

Median SFH: $650K–$750K

Range reflects recent Zillow ZHVI data · live figure loads when available · see the full market table

Lathrop offers the lowest entry prices of any Bay Area-adjacent city: typical single-family homes run $650K–$750K, anchored by the massive River Islands master-planned community rising along the San Joaquin River. Most of the housing stock is newer, and family-oriented charter schools draw first-time-buyer households.

Straddling I-5 and I-205 with ACE rail service, Lathrop works for commuters heading west over the Altamont — and its logistics-heavy job base keeps rental demand deep.

EDUCATION

Schools in Lathrop

Manteca Unified & Banta Unified School Districts Most of Lathrop falls under Manteca Unified, while the River Islands area is served by Banta Unified — home to the well-regarded River Islands charter academies. Verify which district serves any specific address.

  • Lathrop High School
  • River Islands Technology Academy
  • River Islands High School
  • Mossdale Elementary School
GETTING AROUND

Commute & Connectivity

I-5 and I-205 run through Lathrop, with ACE rail at the Lathrop/Manteca station and a second North Lathrop station planned.

  • ACE train from Lathrop/Manteca station to Fremont, Santa Clara, and San Jose (~2 hrs)
  • Dublin/Pleasanton BART: ~35–45 min via I-205/I-580
  • Pleasanton/Dublin: ~40–50 min drive; Stockton: ~15–20 min
  • North Lathrop ACE/Valley Rail station planned, plus proposed Valley Link to BART
NEIGHBORHOODS

Where to Look in Lathrop

River Islands

The flagship: ~4,800 acres with 11 lakes, the Boathouse Restaurant, and charter academies — building toward ~11,000 homes.

Mossdale Village

Planned community west of I-5 along the river, built around a pedestrian village center with parks and trails.

Central Lathrop

The city's newest growth zone — home to Lathrop High School with newer subdivisions going in.

INVESTOR LENS

Lathrop for Investors

Entry prices are the lowest of any Bay-adjacent city and rental demand is strong from commuters and logistics workers, so cash flow pencils better here than in most of the greater Bay Area. Favor resales over new builds to avoid Community Facilities District (Mello-Roos) special taxes on carrying costs, and target 3–4 bedroom family homes near the charter schools and commuter corridors.
