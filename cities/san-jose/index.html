🏙️ CITY GUIDE

Living in San Jose, California

South Bay schools, bigger lots, and one last price break before the Peninsula

Median SFH: $1.5M–$2.5M

Range reflects recent Zillow ZHVI data · live figure loads when available · see the full market table

San Jose is America's largest city by the measure that matters to buyers — and its southern and eastern neighborhoods (Evergreen, Blossom Valley, Almaden Valley) offer something increasingly rare: top-rated public schools, larger lots, and single-family homes from around $1.5M, a meaningful break from Peninsula prices.

This guide focuses on the three South/East San Jose areas most relevant to relocating families: Evergreen's master-planned hillsides, Blossom Valley's established ranch neighborhoods, and Almaden Valley's premium school zones.

Top schools

Evergreen Valley High and Leland High rank among the Bay Area's best public high schools.

Relative value

Blossom Valley and Evergreen from ~$1.5M — a last price break before the Peninsula.

Transit options

Berryessa BART, Caltrain, and VTA Blue Line connect South San Jose across the region.

Job proximity

Most Santa Clara County tech campuses sit 25–35 minutes away.

EDUCATION

Schools in San Jose

Evergreen, Oak Grove, East Side Union & San Jose Unified School boundaries here are split: Evergreen pairs Evergreen SD (K-8) with East Side Union HSD; Blossom Valley pairs Oak Grove SD with East Side Union HSD; Almaden Valley is mostly San Jose Unified. Evergreen Valley High and Leland High are the headliners — always verify a specific address's assignment.

  • Evergreen Valley High School
  • Leland High School
  • Oak Grove High School
  • Graystone Elementary School
GETTING AROUND

Commute & Connectivity

South San Jose has the Bay Area's full transit menu: BART, Caltrain, VTA light rail, and five freeways.

  • Berryessa/North San Jose BART (opened 2020) with East Bay service; Phase II extension in early construction
  • Caltrain at Blossom Hill, Capitol, and Diridon; VTA Blue Line through South San Jose
  • US-101, I-680, I-280, CA-87, and CA-85 serve all three focus areas
  • Palo Alto/Santa Clara campuses: ~25–35 min; San Francisco: ~50–70 min off-peak
NEIGHBORHOODS

Where to Look in San Jose

Evergreen

Master-planned hillside community in the southeast foothills — newer homes, Evergreen Valley High, and Evergreen Valley College.

Blossom Valley

Established mid-century ranch neighborhoods in South San Jose with Oak Grove schools and strong VTA Blue Line access.

Almaden Valley

Affluent southwest valley with Leland High schools, larger lots, and Almaden Quicksilver trails — the premium tier at $2.3M–$2.5M.

INVESTOR LENS

San Jose for Investors

Cash flow is thin at these price-to-rent ratios — South San Jose is an appreciation-and-stability play, not a cash-flow market. Tenant demand is deep and durable, driven by Silicon Valley tech employment within commuting range, with Almaden commanding premium rents and Evergreen/Blossom Valley offering more accessible entry points. Historically, San Jose homes have roughly doubled each decade over the last 40 years.
